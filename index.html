Key Lime Pie Recipe

This is a super easy key lime pie recipe, it only needs four ingredients and only follows six easy steps to get your delicious key lime pie!


Ingredients: Directions:
  1. 5 Large egg yolks (beaten)
  2. 1 (14 ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
  3. 1/2 cup key lime juice
  4. 1 (9-inch) prepared grahm cracker crust
  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F.
  3. Combine sweetened condensed mil, key lime juice, and egg yolks in a large bowl and mix well.
  4. Pour mixture into unbaked grahm cracker crust.
  5. Bake in preheated oven until filling is set, about 15 minutes.
  6. Allow to cool before slicing.

After finishing all of that, your recipe should be fully complete and you should be left with a delicious key lime pie!
